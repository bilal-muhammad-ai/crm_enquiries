## Do You Check My Items Before Delivery?

**Question:** Do you check my items before delivery?

**Answer:** Glancy Fawcett's experienced Logistics teams, based in Manchester, England and Dublin, Ireland, work to the highest standards of quality control.

**Quality control process:**

1. Every product arriving at their warehouses undergoes extensive quality control.
2. Each item is unpacked, inspected, and then repacked.
3. Any product not meeting required standards is flagged to the manufacturer for discussion and, if necessary, replaced.

They aim to ensure all products meet their high standards and the manufacturer's standards, so items arrive at their destination with the quality you would expect.

For full details, request the **Service Levels document**.

## How Will You Pack My Order?

**Question:** How will you pack my order?

**Answer:** Once all items have passed quality control, everything is packed carefully for export to minimise breakages.

**Packing procedure:**

- Products packed **by room or area** with clearly designated box labels.
- Delivery and packing notes ensure clarity and inventory controls.
- Designed for easy unpacking at the destination.

## Do You Deliver Worldwide?

**Question:** Do you deliver worldwide?

**Answer:** Yes. Glancy Fawcett delivers worldwide using trusted logistic partners to ensure safe and timely delivery of all goods.

| Delivery Type | Method |
|---------------|--------|
| Large or high-value deliveries | Dedicated delivery service |
| Smaller consignments | Global partners such as UPS or DHL for door-to-door delivery |
