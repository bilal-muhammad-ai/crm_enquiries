## How Do We Begin?

**Question:** How do we begin a project with Glancy Fawcett?

**Answer:** Your journey starts with a conversation with one of their Client Relationship Managers, who will consult with you to understand your requirements and vision.

---

## Do You Sign NDAs?

**Question:** Do you sign NDAs?

**Answer:** Yes. Glancy Fawcett is happy to sign NDAs and requires their suppliers to do the same, safeguarding your confidentiality.

**Tags:** nda, confidentiality, legal

## How Do You Take My Brief?

**Question:** How do you take my brief?

**Answer:** Glancy Fawcett will arrange a meeting to carefully understand your design preferences, lifestyle requirements and project details.

---

## Where Will the Brief-Taking Happen?

**Question:** Where will the brief-taking happen?

**Answer:**

- **In-person:** At the UK or UAE showroom, or a location convenient to you.
- **Remote:** Via phone, email, or video conferencing (including virtual showroom tours).

They can also travel to attend meetings — details are outlined in their Service Levels document, available on request.

---

## Introduction to the Wider GF Team

**Question:** What happens after the initial brief?

**Answer:** Having listened carefully to your brief, your Project Manager will create a personalised presentation of luxury products, specific to you. At this stage, you may be looking simply for inspiration, so a general presentation by product type may be most appropriate. Alternatively, they can tailor it precisely to your needs.

As your enquiry develops, Glancy Fawcett will introduce you to their wider team of specialists, including your dedicated Project Manager — one of your main contacts throughout. They handle all communication with brand partners and are both product and project experts, ensuring a streamlined experience.

---

## What Will I Receive Following Our Brief-Taking Session?

**Question:** What will I receive following our brief-taking session?

**Answer:** You will receive a personalised presentation of luxury products tailored to your project.

This may be either:

- **Inspirational** — a general product overview, or
- **Tailored** — a carefully curated selection aligned with your preferences.

Presentations can be delivered digitally or as a beautifully bound printed version.

## How Long Should I Allow for a Showroom Visit?

**Question:** How long should I allow for a showroom visit?

**Answer:** Typically two days. This depends on project scope:

- A smaller home or private jet can be completed in a day.
- More palatial projects may require up to a week.

---

## Can You Work with Short Lead Times?

**Question:** Can you work with short lead times?

**Answer:** Yes. Part of Glancy Fawcett's expertise is knowing which luxury brands carry stock and meet your project brief. Contact them with your ideal delivery dates — they will always do their best to meet your requirements.

---

## How Long Does a Typical Project Take to Deliver?

**Question:** How long does a typical project take to deliver?

**Answer:** This varies significantly by project. Some projects can be delivered in as little as **eight weeks**; others can take **years**. Factors include:

- Project size
- Number of rooms
- Whether products are custom or branded
- At what point Glancy Fawcett was introduced to the project

Glancy Fawcett is happy to discuss a time estimate for your specific project.
