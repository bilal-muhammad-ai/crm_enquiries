## Tagline

Curators of exquisite lifestyles. Unrivalled outfitting of superyachts, residences, and private aircraft with the world's finest tableware, linens, and accessories.

## Who We Are

Glancy Fawcett is a global project-based outfitter, curating, sourcing and delivering luxury tableware, linens and accessories. For over 36 years, the company has been entrusted with outfitting the world's most exceptional spaces.

## Track Record

- More than **650 superyachts** outfitted
- More than **350 private residences** outfitted
- More than **140 aircraft** outfitted
- Over **60 experts** on the team
- Over **36 years** of expertise
- Over **550 brand partners**
- Extensive plate and linen sample libraries

## Value Proposition — Luxury Lifestyle Simplified

Glancy Fawcett simplifies the complexities of luxury living through a seamless, highly considered service built on consistency, discretion and expertise. As a trusted, long-term partner, they support clients across their entire property portfolio, ensuring quality and continuity wherever they are in the world.

**Expert team. The most exclusive brands. Exceptional service — wherever you call home.**

## Client Journey

Your journey begins with a private consultation with the Glancy Fawcett team who take the time to understand your vision. You are then introduced to product experts, who remain by your side throughout the project and beyond.

The team understands the emotional importance of creating beautiful spaces, as well as the complexities of coordinating across multiple properties, time zones and teams. Their role is to manage every detail seamlessly — so the experience feels effortless and the result feels exactly right.

**Goal:** Absolute ease and complete satisfaction.
