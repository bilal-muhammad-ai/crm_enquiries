## Who Designs Your Products?

**Question:** Who designs your products?

**Answer:** In addition to offering products from the world's leading brands, Glancy Fawcett has an in-house Product Design Team that designs and creates products uniquely for you — products called **'Designed by GF'**.

## Tell Me More About 'Designed by GF'

**Question:** What is Designed by GF?

**Answer:** Designed by GF is Glancy Fawcett's exclusive product design experience. With your guidance, they design and create unique products especially for you. Drawing on the knowledge and experience of their in-house Product Design team, they work with your design tastes, concepts and interior style renders to help make your vision a reality.

You are very much part of this process, guiding a team of talented and creative product designers who have helped bring incredible designs and products to life.

**Question:** What items can be made? Can I start from scratch?

**Answer:** Glancy Fawcett can alter most products to your taste or create a new product just for you.

## The Design Journey and How Designs Are Presented

**Question:** Tell me about the design journey and how designs are presented.

**Answer:** Using multiple artistic techniques, the team listens to your style preferences and ideas and begins the design journey with you.

**Process stages:**

1. Research your thoughts and transform them into hand sketches, pattern ideas, or motifs.
2. Take your feedback to create 2D artwork, final renders and design concepts.
3. Use in-house 3D printer and embroidery machine for physical design representations.

The design process is entirely personal to you — you guide the team on your preferred presentation method.

---

## Can You Add My Project's Motif to Existing Designs?

**Question:** Can you add my project's motif to existing designs?

**Answer:** Yes. Through computer software and physical sampling, Glancy Fawcett can help find the perfect position for your motif on existing or new products.

## Do I Need to Visit for This Service?

**Question:** Do I need to visit you for the Designed by GF service?

**Answer:** Glancy Fawcett always recommends visiting in person, as the best experience is seeing and handling the products you wish to purchase. They can also work with you at a convenient location, or remotely if needed — whatever suits you best.
